# PEACOCK – Customer User Manual

Welcome to **Peacock – Smart Wedding Reception Management System**. This guide provides complete walkthroughs for managing your wedding reception.

## 1. Getting Started
1. Open your browser and navigate to `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
2. Click **Register** on the top navigation bar to create your customer account.
3. Once registered, log in to access your personalized **Customer Portal**.

## 2. Planning Your Reception (Booking Wizard)
1. Go to **New Booking** in the sidebar.
2. Select your dream venue (e.g., *The Grand Peacock Ballroom*, *Inle Serene Lakeside Garden*).
3. Choose a curated luxury package (e.g., *Imperial Gold Royal Package*).
4. Pick your event date, reception hours (e.g., 17:00 – 22:00), and expected guest count.
5. Select optional master vendors (Photography, Florist, DJ, Makeup Artist).
6. Confirm your booking request. Your reservation status will be submitted as **Pending** for administrative review.

## 3. Digital Invitation Suite
1. Navigate to **Invitations** in the sidebar.
2. Choose from **13 exclusive stationery designs** (e.g., *Royal Rose & Baroque Gold*, *Classic Champagne Gold*, *Emerald Garden Botanical*).
3. Customize the couple's title, personalized invitation message, and venue directions.
4. Click **Save & Generate Invitation Link**.
5. Copy your shareable link (`/invitation/<token>`) and send it to your guests via Viber, Telegram, or Messenger.
6. When guests open the link, they can read your personalized message and submit their RSVP with dietary preferences directly into your guest dashboard.

## 4. Seating Planner & Reception Tables
1. Click **Seating Planner** in the sidebar.
2. Add tables by number, custom name, and seat capacity (e.g., *Table 1 - VIP Bride Family, Capacity: 10*).
3. Assign RSVP-accepted guests to specific table seats.
4. The system validates capacity in real time to prevent overfilling.

## 5. Budget Tracking & Expenses
1. Click **Budget Tracker** in the sidebar.
2. Set your total reception target budget.
3. Log itemized expenses across categories (Venue, Package, Vendors, Attire, Decor).
4. Monitor spent percentages and remaining available balance in real time.

## 6. Payments via KBZPay (KPay)
1. Navigate to **Payments** in the sidebar.
2. Review your total wedding account cost and outstanding balance.
3. Follow the official KBZPay transfer instructions displayed on screen.
4. Make the payment transfer to the official merchant phone (`09450012345`).
5. Enter your amount, payment method, transaction reference ID, and upload a screenshot of your payment receipt.
6. Once submitted, administrators will audit the receipt and update your balance to **Verified**.

## 7. Dual Theme Support
- Click the sun/moon icon in the upper-right navigation bar to toggle between **Light Mode**, **Dark Mode**, and **System Default** at any time.
