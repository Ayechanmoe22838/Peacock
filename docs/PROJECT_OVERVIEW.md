# PEACOCK – Project Overview

## 1. Executive Summary
**Peacock – Smart Wedding Reception Management System** is a centralized, database-driven web platform designed to streamline and elevate the entire wedding reception planning lifecycle in Myanmar. By integrating venue reservations, bespoke wedding packages, vendor selection, gourmet catering, digital invitations, interactive seating arrangements, comprehensive budget tracking, and secure KBZPay payment records, Peacock transforms a traditionally fragmented process into a unified, elegant digital experience.

## 2. Problem Statement
Traditional wedding planning in Myanmar often suffers from:
1. **Scattered Logistics:** Couples coordinate venues, florists, videographers, caterers, and MCs through fragmented phone calls and messaging chats without a single source of truth.
2. **Double-Booking Risks:** Venues and service providers lack synchronized date-availability locking mechanisms, leading to catastrophic scheduling conflicts.
3. **Budget Leakage:** Without real-time expense and payment tracking, couples face unexpected cost overruns and manual receipt discrepancies.
4. **Manual Guest & Seating Chaos:** Paper RSVP lists and physical seating cards result in overcapacity tables, duplicate assignments, and untracked dietary requirements.
5. **Payment Audit Gaps:** Deposits transferred via mobile wallets are difficult to track, reconcile, and verify against changing booking balances.

## 3. Project Objectives
1. **Centralize Planning:** Consolidate every dimension of reception logistics into an intuitive, responsive web application.
2. **Prevent Scheduling Overlaps:** Enforce strict database transactions and availability locking to eliminate double-booking.
3. **Empower Couples:** Provide customers with self-service tools for budgeting, digital invitations, seating charts, and instant RSVP tracking.
4. **Transparent Payments:** Support KBZPay mobile money transfers with clear receipt uploading and administrative verification workflows.
5. **Auditable Administration:** Offer super administrators real-time business intelligence, dynamic Chart.js analytics, and one-click PDF/CSV management reporting.
