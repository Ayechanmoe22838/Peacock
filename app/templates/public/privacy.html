Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

Peacock collects personal details provided during registration and booking including your name, contact email, phone number, wedding date, guest records, and transaction receipts.

2. Payment & Receipt Security

Payment verification receipts submitted via KBZPay are encrypted and stored safely. Payment receipts are accessible solely to the booking customer and authorized platform administrators. We never store payment PINs, OTPs, or bank passwords.

3. Guest List Confidentiality

Guest lists, RSVP statuses, and table seating arrangements belong strictly to the reserving customer. Guests can only view and update their individual invitation RSVP via secure unguessable tokens.
