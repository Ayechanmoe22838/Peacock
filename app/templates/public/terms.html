Terms and Conditions

Effective date: November 2026

1. Booking & Reservation Policy

A submitted booking request is initially in 'Pending' status. Venue dates are formally locked only once the booking is reviewed by administrators and the required initial deposit is verified.

2. Double-Booking Prevention

Peacock operates an automated conflict-prevention engine. Two approved or confirmed bookings cannot overlap in date and time for the same physical venue.

3. Cancellation & Refunds

Cancellation requests must be submitted through the customer portal. Upon administrator approval, the scheduled venue time slot is released.
