About Peacock

Smart Wedding Reception Management System designed for modern couples and wedding professionals.

Our Mission

Making Wedding Reception Planning Joyful, Elegant & Stress-Free

Wedding planning often involves juggling dozens of venues, caterers, decorators, guest lists, and financial records across spreadsheets and message threads.

Peacock centralizes the entire journey into a single intelligent web application. From reserving magnificent ballrooms without double-booking conflicts, to generating digital wedding invitations, seating guests at interactive tables, and tracking real-time Myanmar Kyat (MMK) expenses with audited KBZPay payment confirmations.
